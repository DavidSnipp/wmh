Sports & recreation

The recreation ground and tennis courts adjoining the hall are home to three long-standing local clubs. New members are always welcome — please get in touch with each club directly.

Cricket

There is a long-standing tradition of cricket on the field. Worplesdon and Burpham Cricket Club traces its formation back to 1890, and its Worplesdon predecessor first used the site in 1912.

Visit the cricket club

Football

Worplesdon Phoenix Football Club plays regular league matches at weekends and occasional games during the week.

Visit the football club

Tennis

Worplesdon Tennis Club was formed in 1991 using the two existing public courts, and has since added two more for club use. A new club house was opened in 2005. The club has a thriving membership of adults and juniors — players of all abilities are welcome.

Visit the tennis club

A sports pavilion serving the cricket, football and tennis clubs was opened in 2008.
